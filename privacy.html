Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

This policy explains how Elementary Watson ("we") handles information in connection with our social media pages and with Elementary Watson Publisher, the internal application we use to publish content to our own accounts.

Who we are

Elementary Watson is a puzzle page. You can reach us at elementarywatson.daily@gmail.com.

What the app does

Elementary Watson Publisher only connects to accounts that we own and manage (Instagram, Facebook, Threads, YouTube, TikTok and Pinterest). It uploads our own images and videos, publishes captions and replies, and reads basic statistics and public comments on our own posts. It does not offer sign-in to other people and does not access anyone else's account.

Information we process

  • Our own account data: account identifiers, access tokens and the content we publish, used only to post and manage our content.
  • Public comments on our posts: when you comment on one of our puzzles, the platform shows us your public username and comment. We may count answers (for example how many people chose each letter) to measure how hard a puzzle was. We do not build profiles, sell data or use it for advertising.
  • Email: if you write to us, we use your address only to reply.

Storage and retention

Access tokens are stored securely on our own systems and are never shared. Comment statistics are kept in aggregate form; individual comments are not stored longer than needed to compute them, and in any case no longer than 12 months.

Sharing

We do not sell or share personal information with third parties. Content is transmitted only to the platforms on which we publish it, under their own terms and privacy policies.

Google and YouTube

Our app uses YouTube API Services to upload videos to and manage our own YouTube channel. By interacting with our YouTube content you are subject to the YouTube Terms of Service, and data handled by Google is governed by the Google Privacy Policy. Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Access granted to our app can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page.

Your rights and data deletion

You can ask us to access or delete any information we hold about you, including statistics derived from your comments, by emailing elementarywatson.daily@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion". We will confirm the deletion within 30 days. You can also delete your comments directly on each platform at any time. If you are in the EU, you may lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.

Children

Our content is intended for a general audience and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date at the top shows the latest version.
